Expected file format

The wide layout written by DataFrame.to_csv / to_parquet for a frame with a 3-level column index and a multi-level row index:

  • Columns (three header rows): date, fly_num, roi. Each column must be unique. roi is either a glomerulus name (D, DA1, … VM7v) or a non-negative integer ROI id.
  • Rows (leading columns): at least odor1 (or odor); optionally panel, is_pair, odor2, …, repeat. Extra odor<n> levels are mixture components presented together in air.
  • Odor strings look like va @ -3: abbreviation, a space, @, a space, log10 concentration (0 or negative). solvent marks an unused component.

A long layout (one row per value, with columns date, fly_num, roi, [panel,] odor1, …, [repeat,] value) is also accepted; parquet files with integer fly_num usually need this layout.

Settings

Who you are and where your data is on tensor, so that the upload form can fill itself in. Your folders are saved on the server under your name; the upload form defaults are kept on this computer only.

You and your data on tensor

Give folders relative to the top of tensor (on the server: …), e.g. tom/raw_data. Each is checked on the server when you leave its field; once all of them are found there, they are saved under your name.

Raw data

ThorImage recordings, as <folder>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<fly>/<recording>/Experiment.xml.

Motion-corrected data

Laid out like the raw data: <folder>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<fly>/…

Odor metadata

Olfactometer YAML files, up to three folders deep. Those named in the notes of a fly's ThorImage recordings, or with one of the flies' dates in their name, are tried for an upload.

Upload form defaults

Filled into the upload form for every new dataset, unless its files (or its data on tensor) give a value. Checked as the upload form checks them when you leave a field, and kept on this computer only.
